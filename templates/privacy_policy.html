Privacy Policy 🔒

Welcome to CloudEats — your privacy, our priority.

Your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit or use our services.

1️⃣ Information We Collect

  • Personal Info: Name, email, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Account Info: Username and password for login.
  • Payment Info: Processed securely via Razorpay; we never store card data.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited and actions performed on our site.

2️⃣ How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your CloudEats account.
  • To process your orders and handle secure payments.
  • To improve our service and personalize your experience.
  • To send updates and special offers (if you opt-in).

3️⃣ Data Security

We use advanced encryption and security measures to protect your data. Passwords are stored in encrypted form. All payments are securely handled through trusted gateways.

4️⃣ Cookies & Sessions 🍪

We use cookies to enhance your experience, manage your shopping cart, and remember your preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not work properly.

5️⃣ Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Information is shared only with payment gateways and service providers required to process your orders securely.

6️⃣ Your Rights

You can update or delete your account anytime. You may also unsubscribe from promotional emails through your account settings.

7️⃣ Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected here with the updated date.

8️⃣ Contact Us 📧

For any questions or privacy concerns, contact our support team:
